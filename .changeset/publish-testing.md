`@bunvex/testing` (`bunvexTest`) is published, with `@bunvex/sandbox`, the function sandbox's runtime it depends on. `@bunvex/helpers` and the components `@bunvex/sharded-counter`, `@bunvex/batch-worker` and `@bunvex/rate-limiter` are published at their own versions, compiled as the other packages (a component's `bunvex` entry is its compiled `bunvex.config.js`; `@bunvex/component-source` keeps its sources).
