# bunvex end to end on a MacBook (M1 Pro, 8 cores, 16 GB) — 29 Sep 2026

Native stores, no Docker VM (`scripts/mac-stores.sh`): Postgres 17 (Homebrew, :5434), MySQL 8.4 (Homebrew,
:3307, `innodb_flush_log_at_trx_commit=1`, `sync_binlog=1`), MongoDB 8.0.32 (official tarball, :27018,
journaled). `scripts/mac-bench.sh` runs the `convex-bench` suite (10 s per point, 8/32/128 VUs) and the
fan-out (100 → 10 000 subscribers) per storage, one at a time. k6 and the generator share the 8 cores.
**macOS `fsync` does not force the drive cache: durable-write numbers are optimistic** (the VPS report is
the reference for writes). Convex column: Convex self-hosted on the same Mac, Postgres, profile `tuned`
(`convex-bench/results/*-mac-postgres-tuned`, 20 s per point).

## Harness fixes this run needed (convex-bench)

- macOS accepts only 128 pending connections per listener (`kern.ipc.somaxconn=128`): bursts of
  handshakes are refused. The bunvex test client now retries (up to 5, with backoff) and never rejects —
  an unhandled rejection used to kill a worker, and the parent then waited for it forever.
- `fanout.mjs` now times out and detects worker death in the CONNECT phase too (`dead_workers` in the
  result), and `run-suite.sh` waits for TIME_WAIT sockets to drain between fan-out sizes and keeps the
  generator's stderr in `fanout-stderr.log`.
